Help / Tutorial

  1. Read the problem and identify the unit you need in the final answer.
  2. Drag a value tile into a numerator or denominator slot.
  3. Use Add Step for extra conversion factors.
  4. Use arrow buttons to reorder steps if your setup needs adjustment.
  5. Select Check cancellation to see crossed-out units and feedback.

Common mistakes

  • Wrong answer unit left over at the end.
  • Forgot a needed conversion factor (for example mg to mcg).
  • Numerator and denominator flipped.
  • Units do not fully cancel.

Rounding note: This app can show rounded results for learning. Your school or instructor may use different rounding rules.
